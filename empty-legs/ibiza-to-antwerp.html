Why this route produces empty legs

This is the return half of the busiest pair in our data. An aircraft that carried passengers down to Ibiza has to get back to its Belgian base, and that flight is empty unless someone takes it.

The trade is the same everywhere: you give up control of the date and often the exact hour, and you fly private for a fraction of the charter price. If your plans are fixed to the hour, ask for a normal quote instead.

Before you book

Ask the operator three things: whether the departure time can still move, what happens if the paid flight that created this empty leg falls through, and which exact airport you are using. On Ibiza to Antwerp that last point matters, because the city name in a listing can mean more than one field.

If nothing is listed today, that is normal. Empty legs on Ibiza to Antwerp appear with days of notice, not months, which is why the alert below beats checking the page by hand. You can also look at Antwerp to Ibiza or Wevelgem to Malaga, which often carry the same aircraft a day earlier or later.
