Empty leg flights in Spain

Spain has 59 upcoming empty legs on our board, 34 leaving and 32 arriving, with 7 flying inside the country. 16 of the 59 carry a published price, so most Spanish cards say price on enquiry.

Ibiza is on 18 legs, more than any other Spanish field, then Málaga at 10, Barcelona at 7, Girona at 6 and Madrid at 6. Look at the other end of the corridors and one name keeps coming up: Wevelgem, the Belgian business airport, to Ibiza four times, to and from Girona, to and from Málaga. The British end is London Luton to Málaga.

Across the 14 legs priced in euros the median aircraft is 6,200 and the median seat 689. The CJ4 Gen 2 is on 14 legs, but Spain also draws bigger cabins than Belgium or France: a Latitude on 6, a Gulfstream V on 4, a Legacy 500 on 3. We have recorded 343 legs touching Spain since the end of July, and the summer islands account for a good share of what has already flown.

Ibiza has more empty legs than Madrid and Barcelona put together. The operators follow the season, not the capital.

How empty legs work in Spain

An empty leg is a flight a charter operator has to make anyway, to position an aircraft for a booking or to bring it home afterwards, sold at a discount because the alternative is flying it empty. The operator sets the route, the date and the aircraft. You fit into a flight that already exists, and it is one way: there is no return leg and no such thing as a return seat.

Everything on this page comes from Spanish legs that operators published on their own websites. We read those sites several times a day and record each leg exactly as listed, route, date, aircraft, seats and the operator's own price where they gave one. We do not read brokers, we take no commission and enquiries go straight to the operator. A leg counts for Spain when either end resolves to a Spanish airport, first by the airport code the operator gave and then by the city name, so a leg written as an airport name still lands in the right country.

The written figures above are a snapshot from September 2026 and are restated, not quietly edited, when we rerun them. The live list is the board as it stands now.

Questions people ask about empty legs in Spain

Which Spanish airport has the most empty legs?

Ibiza, on 18 of the 59 upcoming empty legs touching Spain, ahead of Málaga at 10, Barcelona at 7, Girona at 6 and Madrid at 6.

How much does an empty leg to Spain cost?

Across the 14 upcoming legs priced in euros the median published price is 6,200 euros for the whole aircraft, 689 euros a seat divided by the listed seats. Most Spanish legs, 43 of 59, do not carry a published price.

Where do empty legs to Ibiza come from?

Belgium more than anywhere else on our board: Wevelgem to Ibiza is the most repeated corridor into Spain, with Antwerp and Paris behind it.
