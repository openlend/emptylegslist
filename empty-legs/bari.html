Empty leg flights from Bari

Bari is the way into Puglia, the heel of Italy: Polignano, Ostuni, the trulli of Alberobello and the coast down to Lecce. It is a summer market, and a young one for private aviation compared with the Amalfi side.

We have not yet recorded an empty leg touching Bari on the board. This page exists so the alert is here when one appears, and because Puglia traffic has been growing every season. If you want this corner of Italy, set an alert rather than checking, and see Naples and Olbia in the meantime.

The airports

  • Bari Karol Wojtyła (BRI) — at Palese, ten kilometres north west of the city.
  • Brindisi (BDS) — an hour and a half south, and closer for Ostuni and Lecce.
  • Grottaglie (TAR) — inland near Taranto, used by general aviation.

Puglia is long. Bari serves the north of it and Brindisi the south, and the drive between them is real, so check which one a listing means.

What an empty leg from Bari costs

Where an operator publishes a price it is shown exactly as listed, as a whole-aircraft figure rather than a per-seat fare. Where none is published the card says so rather than estimating.

Northern European sectors to Puglia are around two and a half hours. Common types include the Citation XLS, the Challenger 350 and the Legacy 500. You can also browse the whole list by aircraft type.

Bari empty legs, by the numbers

Between 31 July and 27 August 2026 we recorded 7 empty leg flights touching Bari: 1 departing, 6 arriving, across 6 partner airports. The airports that came up most were Wevelgem (2), Birmingham (1), Brindisi (1), Dubrovnik (1). 2 different aircraft types appeared, most often the Citation CJ4 Gen 2 (2), the Embraer Legacy 500 (2).

Seven legs and only one of them leaving. People seem to arrive in Bari and stay.

These are counts from our own board over that window, so they reflect the operators we read rather than the whole market, and they are a record of what was listed, not a forecast. New legs appear with days of notice, which is what the route alert is for.

What to know before you enquire

An empty leg exists because of the paid charter around it, so it can be sold or moved without notice. Every listing here is recorded from what the operator published, with the date we last saw it. Treat it as a strong indication rather than a guarantee, and confirm with the operator before planning around it.
