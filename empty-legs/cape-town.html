What to know about Cape Town

The northbound direction is where the value sits: everything flies south loaded during the southern summer and returns with nobody aboard. Add the Garden Route and the winelands and you get a steady flow of shorter regional legs too, often on turboprops that can use the smaller fields.

Every listing is recorded from what the operator published, and each one carries the date we last saw it. An empty leg exists because of the paid charter around it, so it can be sold or moved without notice. Treat what you see here as a strong indication rather than a guarantee, and confirm the detail with the operator before you plan around it.
