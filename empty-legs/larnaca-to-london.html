Why this route produces empty legs

Cyprus to London is a four and a half hour sector, which makes an empty leg on it unusually valuable. Operators fly clients out to Larnaca and need the aircraft back in London, so the return goes on the board.

The trade is the same everywhere: you give up control of the date and often the exact hour, and you fly private for a fraction of the charter price. If your plans are fixed to the hour, ask for a normal quote instead.

Before you book

Ask the operator three things: whether the departure time can still move, what happens if the paid flight that created this empty leg falls through, and which exact airport you are using. On Larnaca to London that last point matters, because the city name in a listing can mean more than one field.

If nothing is listed today, that is normal. Empty legs on Larnaca to London appear with days of notice, not months, which is why the alert below beats checking the page by hand. You can also look at London to Larnaca or London to Paris, which often carry the same aircraft a day earlier or later.
