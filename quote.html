Charter Questions, Answered

How much does a private jet charter cost?

Price depends on aircraft type, route, and season. See our breakdown of private jet cost per hour and the full guide to private jet costs.

What is the cheapest way to charter?

Empty leg flights and smaller aircraft lower the price. Our guide to the most affordable way to charter a private jet covers the options, and this list of affordable private jets shows which aircraft cost less to fly.

Do operators accept pets?

Many operators do, with advance notice; confirm with each operator. Read our guide on private jet charter with pets and mention your pet in the notes field above.

Is my request binding?

No. You receive quotes and decide. Booking happens directly between you and the operator.
