List your empty legs

List your empty legs where people are actively looking for them. It is free, we do not take a commission, and every enquiry goes straight to you.

Last updated 1 August 2026

  • Free to list. No fee, no commission, no contract.
  • Enquiries are forwarded to you with the client's own contact details.
  • You keep the pricing. We publish the figure you publish.
  • Your name is not shown on the public listing during this phase.

How it works

  1. You send us the pageThe simplest route: give us the URL of the empty legs page on your own website. We read it three times a day and pick up changes automatically, so you never have to tell us anything twice.
  2. We check itA person looks at the first import: airports, dates, aircraft, seats and price. If the fields do not map cleanly we come back to you rather than guessing.
  3. It goes liveYour flights appear in the list, on the matching route pages and on the page for that aircraft type. Each listing carries the date we last saw it.
  4. Enquiries reach youWhen someone enquires on one of your flights we send you their name, email, phone if given, and what they asked for. You quote them directly, on your terms.
  5. It stays currentSold or cancelled legs drop off as soon as they leave your page. If you would rather send a feed or a file instead of a web page, tell us the format and we will work with it.

What we need from you

Per flight: departure airport, arrival airport, date, aircraft type, seats, and price if you publish one. Anything else you send we will carry. Anything missing we will show as missing rather than filling the gap.

Removing a listing

Ask and it comes down, same day, no questions. We record the source of every listing, so a removal takes one message. If you would rather we never read your site at all, say so and we will exclude your domain.

We use what you send only to set up and run your listing. See the privacy notice.
