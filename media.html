What Empty Leg List is

An empty leg is a one way sector an aircraft flies with nobody on board, because it is repositioning between paid charters. That flight is happening either way, so the operator would rather sell it cheaply than fly it empty.

The problem was never the flights. It was finding them. They appear with days of notice, they are published across dozens of separate operator websites, and they disappear when they sell. Empty Leg List reads what operators publish, three times a day, and puts it in one place with the operator's own price attached.

It is a listing service. Not a broker, not an operator, not a party to any booking, and it takes no commission. An enquiry goes to the operator that published the flight. There is a fuller explanation on the about page.

Ways to work together

Featured operator

Priority visibility for operators who want their empty legs found faster, across the city, route and aircraft pages that match their fleet.

Own a corridor. Branded presence around the discovery and the alerts for a route you care about.

Destination partner

Hotels, villas and tourism brands placed in front of people already looking at flights to that destination, rather than at generic travel traffic.
