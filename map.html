How to read it

Each line is one empty leg, drawn from the airport it leaves to the airport it lands at. Gold means the operator published a price for the whole aircraft; a dashed white line means the listing says price on enquiry. The lines are one way, because an empty leg is an aircraft repositioning and the operator sells that single flight, not a return.

Clusters mean an operator is busy in that area. Teterboro, Van Nuys, Miami, Nice and London are where most lines start or end. A single line across an ocean is usually a long range jet going home.

Click a city dot for the flights touching it and the page we keep for that city. The map refreshes with the board, several times a day. To hear about a route rather than watch for it, set a route alert. The same data as a table is on the live list, and as a file on open data.
