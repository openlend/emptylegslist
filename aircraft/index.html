Why the type matters

An empty leg is a repositioning flight, so you are taking the aircraft the operator already has moving. That means the cabin, the range and the runway it can use are fixed before you book. A turboprop reaches a short regional strip a jet cannot; a heavy jet gives you thirteen seats for close to midsize money when it happens to be flying your way. Picking by type is the fastest way to filter a list like this.
