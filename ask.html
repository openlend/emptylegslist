Reading the board.

Prices are the operator's own figure for the whole aircraft, as published, in the currency the operator used. The price filter compares those figures without converting between currencies, so a euro and a dollar count the same here. Every row links to the flight's own page.

What it can read

Places, as "Nice to London", "from Geneva", "out of Milan", "to Ibiza" or just a city name. Dates, as "today", "tomorrow", "next week", "in 10 days", "next 2 weeks", "12 September", "2026-12-01" or a month name on its own. Seats, as "6 seats", "party of 4", "eight people". Price, as "under 10,000", "over 15,000", "cheap" or "with a price". A figure you name is applied as a real cap or floor on the published price, and it is shown in the Price up to and Price from boxes so you can see it and change it. Currencies are compared as published, without conversion. Aircraft, by any type on the board, as "Citation XLS" or "Praetor 600". A region, as "Europe" or "North America", when no city is named.

Anything it could not read is shown back to you in red, so nothing is dropped silently. The controls above always show what it decided, and you can correct any of them and search again.
